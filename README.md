I'm a maker in a new chapter — teaching myself to build software and shipping it in public.
I like **small, native, local-first** tools: things that live quietly in your menu bar, do one
thing well, and feel at home on macOS. I build a lot of them with AI agents as a co-pilot, and
I care about the details — Apple HIG, calm typography, no noise.

### 🛠️ How I build

- **Small on purpose** — if it makes the screen busier, it probably doesn't ship.
- **Local-first** — your data stays on your machine.
- **Native & calm** — SwiftUI / Electron, HIG-respecting, quiet by default.
- **In public** — rough edges and all. Feedback and PRs are always welcome.
